Work Demand, one step at a time
Updated 3 November 2026. Sample records only.

What you will learn
Let's follow two workers as they ask for five days of work. You'll see what to do on the phone, and what the office does on the computer. Every name and date you see is for practice.

Download and sign in
Use the download link below the video. Choose Android for your phone, or Windows or Linux for your computer.

Download and sign in
On the computer, start with Sign in with Google.

Choose your area
On Select login area, choose your State and Financial Year. The Financial Year is the year for these work records. Then choose your District and Block. Check that all four choices are right. Click Save and open login page.

Choose your area
Sign in to the work website with your own account. You enter the Security Code and choose Login.

Choose your area
Open My Areas to keep your usual Panchayats and Villages together. When records need to be read, the app starts with the area you are using.

Send a request from Android
The office sets up phone access under Android access. Get your private login code from the office.

Send a request from Android
On the phone, check your area. Tap Create work request. Find the workers who asked for work. Tick one name at a time.

Send a request from Android
Both workers want five days. Type five in Days for all selected workers. Tap Apply Days to All. Check that both workers now show five days.

Send a request from Android
Tap Continue. Choose the Start Date. Then open Review request.

Send a request from Android
Check the names, days, and dates. Tap Send to office. Open History to see the request. The office still needs to check it.

Review a request at the office
On the computer, open Work Demand. Choose Received requests. Open a request. Read the names, requested days, and start dates.

Review a request at the office
Click Check demand. Read the result before you do anything else. If a worker needs a closer look, read the reason beside their name.

Create a request at the office
You can also start a request on the computer. Choose Create demand. Check the Financial Year. Then choose your Panchayat and Village.

Create a request at the office
Open Workers and tick the names you want. You can use Worker filters to narrow the list.

Create a request at the office
Add a Work Code or Work Order if you need one. These tell the app which work you mean. If the Work step is optional, you can leave it blank.

Create a request at the office
Open Days and dates. The Application Date is when the workers asked for work. Check that date first. Enter five in No of Days. Use Start Date for the first day the workers want to work. The app shows the End Date. Read it, then click Check demand.

Create a request at the office
Use Print Demand if you want a draft copy for review or printing. A draft copy lets you review the names, days, and dates before official entry.

Read the saved result
Read the checked list. In the real office workflow, Send to V. B. Gram G. starts official entry. Watch the progress. You may still have to press a final Submit button on the work website.

Read the saved result
Look at these two counts. Two entry steps finished. The app could confirm only one saved demand. When a result is unclear, check the saved list before trying to send it again.

Find reports and settings
Open Reports for Muster Rolls. A Muster Roll is the worker attendance list. Choose the Panchayat and the Villages you want.

Find reports and settings
Choose the groups to include. Use Generate to make the PDF.

Find reports and settings
In Settings you can change the look of the app and manage saved records. Open Balance and recharges to see your balance.

Try it yourself
Open the practice app below the video. Pick two workers, enter five days, and click Check demand. Take your time. You can replay any lesson.
