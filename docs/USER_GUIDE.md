## 1. Install and sign in

### First setup for your app account

Sign in to the app with your own Google account. This is separate from the official portal sign-in. Portal setup has only two steps:

1. **Select login area.** Choose your State, Financial Year, District and Block from the current portal lists. Choose **Save and open login page**. Use **Change login area** if you need another area.
2. **Sign in.** Enter your User ID, password and Security Code on the official VBGRAMG page. Choose **Login** yourself.

The app waits for the login form to be ready. Images that are still loading do not stop it. If the portal cannot open your choices, read the message below **Save and open login page**. Your area stays saved. Use the same button to try again. If a Security Code page is already open, finish it in **Sign in**.

After a confirmed sign-in, **Work Demand → Create demand** opens straight away. Choose the area and workers for the demand there. The first time, a short **Welcome tour** opens on top: six small screens about signing in, choosing your area, automatic downloads, making and checking a Work Demand, and Received requests. Use **Next** and **Back**, or the dots, to move. **Skip tour** or **Start using the app** closes it for good; **Open the full guide** shows the detailed 7-step guide. To see the tour again, open **Settings → Help** and choose **Take the welcome tour**, or choose **Welcome tour** inside **Quick guide**. Setup does not ask for work areas, download records or start Muster Roll report downloads. Older unfinished setup downloads do not block you.

After the tour, a new installation shows a small **Getting started** card with **Step N of 7**, one sentence about what to do next, and **Show me**. On a wide window it sits at the foot of the left menu. On a narrow window it sits in a bottom corner, and in **Browser** it sits above the portal page, never on top of it. **Show me** opens the right screen, dims the rest of the page and points at the button or box with a gold ring. The pointed place still works. Choose **Got it** or press Esc to close it. Each step ticks itself when the app sees the work done: the login area saved, a confirmed portal sign-in, a Panchayat and Village chosen, the Village's records ready, workers with their days, a successful **Check demand**, and a finished entry with its result. Choosing **Show me** never ticks a step. The minus button makes the card smaller. **Hide guide** hides it on this computer; open it again in **Settings → Help & tips → Show Getting started guide**. A computer that used the app before this card was added does not show it on its own.

**Save my User ID and password securely after a successful sign-in** is selected on the login-area form. Clear it if you do not want to save them. Only a confirmed sign-in saves the original pair in the computer's secure storage. A failed login keeps the earlier saved pair. The Security Code is never saved. Browser **Save login details**, **Update login details** and **Login details** remain available. Choose **Hide** to give the portal page more room, and the 🔑 button in the Browser toolbar to show the login bar again. The app remembers this choice on the PC.

All app menus stay available before portal sign-in and after portal sign-out. Opening a menu does not sign you in or submit records. Portal checks and entry still need the right signed-in account. Selected workers must pass the required checks before an official demand is sent.

### Records for your selected area

Choose the area you need in **Work Demand**. The app reuses saved records and reads missing records for that work. You can also use **My Areas** for places you work on often, or **Settings → Records** to download or update area records. These tools are separate from portal setup. **Reports** opens the Muster Roll tools when you need them. A Muster Roll is the official worker attendance list.

The current Panchayat or Village gets the first download and check. Completed records stay saved. A deliberate pause is kept. In Settings, **Download Data** reuses current saved records; **Update Data** asks for new checks. Read the progress message if a report stops. Personal sign-in and report Security Codes still need your answer.

Worker details and the four required checks come first. When Aadhaar Authentication, Aadhaar Seeding, ABPS and eKYC are all Yes, the app shows **Aadhaar Availability: Yes** so you can prepare Work Demand. It keeps the source of this answer. An actual No from the portal replaces that answer and blocks that worker. Download completion and permission to send a demand stay separate.

After all required members are read, **Aadhaar Availability download completed** gives separate counts for **Yes**, **No**, and **No answer selected on the portal**. No and unselected answers count as downloaded. Those workers need a confirmed Yes before Work Demand; other eligible workers can continue. An unread answer stays unknown. Reusing an answer keeps its original check date.

### Start the whole app again

Open **Settings → Factory reset** in the desktop app. This removes downloaded data, all settings, areas, filters, drafts, local request copies, local activity and entry history, recovery copies, saved passwords and sign-ins for every app account on this computer. Export anything you need first. The reset cannot be undone in the app.

Choose **Factory reset**, read the confirmation, tick **I understand that all local app data will be deleted**, then choose **Reset app and restart**. Cancel keeps your data. Work Demand entry or another active portal task must finish or stop before resetting. Report files must finish saving first. If another copy of the app has these records open, close that copy and its command window, then try again. This check happens before reset removes any records.

The app stops downloads, closes its local service, deletes the local stores and clears Browser sign-ins, then creates an empty record store and reopens at first setup. Sign in to the app again, choose your login area and sign in to the portal. Work Demand Creation opens next. Download area records when you need them. If reset is interrupted, reopening finishes it before normal startup. If a file cannot be removed, the app explains the problem instead of claiming a fresh start.

Your online account, online balance, online requests and official VBGRAMG records stay in place. Online records can appear again after you sign in. Files you exported outside the app stay in place. The saved storage drive stays the same. A shared/server database or a linked data folder needs help from the app manager; this button does not erase it. The web preview cannot reset the installed app.

Source-report downloads do not submit a Work Demand or generate a paid PDF copy. PDF saving and printing keep their existing separate confirmation and Balance rules.

If Job Cards, members, eKYC, ABPS, Aadhaar seeding and Aadhaar authentication are already saved and current, the selected area's missing Aadhaar Availability can be read directly from Work Demand. The app keeps those saved details. It skips confirmed Aadhaar Availability Yes answers and reads only the Job Cards that still need an answer. A complete selected Village also keeps its saved area list. Missing, changed or old records still get the reads they need.

### Update the Windows app

Choose **Update now** once. The app downloads and checks the file, then closes. A small installer window shows the update being installed, and the updated app opens by itself when it finishes. You do not need to click anything else, and you do not need to open the app yourself. Your settings, selected areas, downloaded records, drafts and securely saved login details are kept. A normal update does not repeat completed first setup or download everything again. Reports that need a daily update are checked as usual. If Windows asks for permission, read the request and allow your app's installer to continue. Do not turn off Windows protection.

If the download or installer cannot start, choose **Try again** in the same visit. The app reuses a downloaded file only after checking it again. **Open current app** remains available for an optional update. A required update must finish before the old app can be used.

**For older installed versions:** Install version 1.4.18 once to receive this improved updater. An older app still controls its own update steps until it is replaced. Linux and Android keep their normal device installation steps.

2. Keep your existing app and saved records when updating. See the release notes for installation checks and known limits.
3. On desktop, use **Sign in with Google** and finish in your computer's usual browser. This is separate from your official portal login.
4. In **Settings → Portal login**, choose your State, Financial Year, District and Block from the current lists. Save the area.
5. Use **Show menu** on a narrow screen to open the app's menus. If an optional menu is hidden, show it in **Settings → Sidebar menus**.

The sidebar shows one line for each tool. Point at a tool to read a short explanation. Below 900 pixels wide, choose the menu button to open the menu as a drawer. **Work Demand** brings Received requests and manual entry together. Use its two source choices to switch without losing your current draft. **Android access** opens your own and Secretary access tools. Choose **Arrange menus** to show, hide or group menus. You can also drag menus into your preferred order. Every menu shows without scrolling. **Collapse menu**, beside the app name, keeps just the icons; **Expand menu** restores the names. Help and account links stay at the bottom, with **Sign out** beside your account name. On a short window the help links show as a row of icons; point at one to see its name.

Your selected Panchayat and Village stay in place while Received requests and background records are checked. Changing a Panchayat updates its Village choices. Use **Update lists** when you need to read an area list again. The earlier choices stay visible during that read and if it fails; choose **Try again** after a failure. Changing a parent area clears only the choices below it.

### Find the next task

Open **Work Demand**. **Your work** shows unfinished Received requests from all days, including requests that need checking. It stays compact while you edit a request; choose **Show work overview** to open the list. The currently selected Village comes first, followed by its Panchayat. Choose **Open request** to review one. A finished request stays separate from an unfinished saved-result check.

Choose **Check problems** to open the app, saved-record, selected-area, portal sign-in and request-list checks. Follow **Open Browser** if sign-in is needed. These checks send no demand. **Review last entry** opens the last saved summary when members still need attention.

During a record download, the request list and open request show a bar and percentage. The percentage uses saved record checks. **Finding download totals** means the complete worker list is still being read. Paused downloads keep their saved progress. Sign-in problems and missing records remain visible. **100%** means the record checks finished; still choose **Check demand** before portal entry.

Downloads are automatic. When you choose a Panchayat or Village, only that area downloads. Other areas wait until it is complete. Job Card and worker details, eKYC, ABPS (Aadhaar Based Payment System), Aadhaar Seeding and Aadhaar Authentication download together. The four worker checks start as soon as the list of Job Cards and workers is saved, while the household pages are still downloading. Days worked and days left come next, then Work Codes, Work Orders and other reports. Aadhaar Availability on the Work Demand form is checked last, and the app shows how many workers it has checked. A worker whose four checks are all Yes counts as **Aadhaar Availability: Yes** for now, until the Work Demand form confirms the answer. If a read stops or fails, the app tries again on its own within two minutes and reuses completed records. If the Job Card page and the attendance report both open but show different work days, both are saved and the area still counts as downloaded. The download card says how many households to check. Reading again would not change them, so the app checks them again with its daily update. You do not need to click a download button. Private checks still need your own portal sign-in.

In the selected area's **Record updates**, each item shows its saved or checked count. Job Cards and workers have separate counts. Days worked shows how many active workers have usable saved days. Work Codes and reported Work Order numbers show separate saved counts for the selected Panchayat. Each identity or payment check shows how many active workers have a current answer, including a reported No. Earlier answers that need an update are shown separately. **Total still being checked** means the complete list is not known yet; saved counts remain visible.

**Required records come first.** The app first saves Job Card numbers or Registration IDs and household and member details from the complete registration and issued-card lists. Matching households appear in groups of up to 16 before their detail pages are requested. It then reads Aadhaar Authentication, Aadhaar Seeding, ABPS and eKYC together. The same worker report can fill missing Gender and Worker ID. Workers with all four Yes can begin Work Demand preparation while day figures and other reports continue. Actual Aadhaar Availability is checked last. Until the selected area's requested records finish, other Panchayats and Villages wait with their progress saved. Slow or failed extra reports do not hold up the basic worker list. Paused downloads remain paused.

Checked households appear as they are saved. The Village remains incomplete until every required page and household is accounted for. Closing a screen or retrying a download keeps completed saves. The active area's requested reports keep priority. Other extra reports start after 15 seconds without activity and pause at the next safe point when you resume work. A report needed for your current **Check demand** keeps its priority.

If a public worker list cannot be read, the app keeps the saved workers and follows its normal retry time. A failed public read is separate from waiting for private worker checks. Open **Record updates** to see what needs another read.

The Job Cards screen reads its list and totals together. Search covers all saved households before choosing a display page, including matching inactive workers and workers whose checks have not passed. Background updates keep the open households, search and page. **Eligible**, **Not eligible**, **Not yet checked** and **Data unavailable** describe the saved worker checks. Missing answers never mean eligible. Always run **Check demand** before portal entry.

Above the worker table, use **Show Worked** and **Show Days left** separately. Both are off until you turn them on. Your choices stay saved when the app reopens. The **Workers with no work this Financial Year** filter is information only and never leaves a worker out. These figures are information only, whether shown or hidden. Missing, old or unfinished day reports do not stop creating or checking a demand, reduce requested days or leave workers out. If both day values are missing, they stay **Not known**. Old figures show **Update needed** on a separate line below the saved number.

The app uses the **125-day Job Card total** to fill a missing value:

- **Days left = 125 − days worked.** For example, 76 worked means 49 left; 10 worked means 115 left.
- **Days worked = 125 − days left.** For example, 76 left means 49 worked.

These days are shared by the whole Job Card. If a member's own worked days are missing, the desktop shows the shared worked figure with **Job Card total** below it. A member's recorded worked days keep their own value. The Village total counts each Job Card once. A calculation keeps the saved check time and does not become a new portal check. Reported values and warnings about disagreeing reports are kept.

A current saved shared total avoids another request just for days left. If the required Aadhaar page already contains a checked day total, the app saves it during that same visit. A separate day check can still run when the available records need verification.

If the complete saved family list has a current worked value for every member, the app can add those values once and calculate the family's days left. One missing member value keeps that sum unknown. This does not give each member a separate 125 days.

The same rule applies to phone requests, Received request approval and **Send to VBGRAMG**. Missing, old, zero or small saved day totals cannot block these steps or reduce the days you entered. Checks for worker identity, required answers, valid dates and repeated or overlapping worker requests still apply. Any message from the official portal must still be resolved there.

In **Create demand**, the search, list size, **Add workers** and member buttons sit below **Optional day information**, directly above the worker list. Use **Add workers** to find saved members and add them to this demand. It remains available when the list is empty.

Use **Sort by** to arrange the whole saved list by name, Job Card number, active first or inactive first. Names and Job Card numbers can run in either order. Job Card groups stay together, and sorting happens before the list is divided into pages. Unknown status is kept separate from confirmed active and inactive status. Your sort choice is remembered. Sorting keeps your selected workers, entered days and dates, and checked demand.

Worker rows keep the last confirmed registration status when its daily check is old. **Saved: Active**, **Saved: Inactive** or **Saved: Deleted** appears with **Update needed**. Point to the status to see when it was checked. This saved label does not replace the current checks used for filters, sorting or demand entry. Records with no confirmed status still show **Active: unknown**.

Each worker has a clear status tag: **Active**, **Inactive** or **Deleted**. Deleted records stay saved and can be found by name or Job Card search. Search also shows matching records left out by your current filters, for review. Change the relevant Worker filter to **No filter** to keep them in the normal list. Required checks still decide whether a worker can be selected. This follows the selected area for any State, Panchayat or Village offered by the portal and the signed-in account; no example place limits the status labels.

Registration status is checked automatically when its daily update is due, while the app is running and downloads are enabled. Failed reads keep the previous saved status and follow the normal retry time. A successful background update changes the displayed tag without pressing **Refresh saved records**, keeping your entered days and dates. A deliberate download pause is respected. A portal or sign-in problem must be resolved before the missing records can finish updating.

Work Demand always checks the correct Job Card number and matching worker details, completed **eKYC**, active **ABPS**, and **Aadhaar Availability: Yes**. These checks cannot be turned off. If the Work Demand form has not confirmed Yes yet, all four matching, current worker checks can supply a temporary Yes for preparation. An actual No or an unanswered question on the form still blocks that worker. A later form answer replaces the temporary value. A confirmed form Yes keeps the existing eKYC and ABPS requirements. Photo and day figures remain optional information. **Ready to check a demand** and **all downloads complete** are separate results.

If Settings cannot read the worker requirements, it shows a message and **Try again**. Required checks stay required. Portal entry still needs a successful demand check.

Leave **Work Order or work** empty when no work is selected. An unfinished work list does not block that demand. A work you select still needs the matching Work Code, area, Financial Year and other existing work checks. The official portal keeps its own checks, and you still choose every official action.

### Protect saved records

Open **Settings → Records → Record backups**. The desktop app tries to make one checked copy each day while open. **Back up records** makes a copy now. **Check latest backup** recovers it into a separate test copy and checks it without replacing current records.

These copies contain this PC's saved Job Cards, workers, works and activity history. Phone requests, submission summaries, login details and government records are separate. Keep both backup files privately on another drive to protect against a lost PC. The app retains seven daily copies and five manual copies. Restoring the app's current records requires closing it and help from the person who manages it.

## 2. Create your personal Android access

On desktop, open **Android access**, then **My phone**.

The panel has two sections: **My login code** and **My areas**. Choose **View my areas** to read the saved list. Area choices and extra help stay closed until needed.

To replace an existing personal code, choose **Choose my own login code** near your current code. Enter **My new login code**, then choose **Save my login code**. Your selected areas stay the same. **Show code** lets you check what you typed. Cancel keeps your current code. The new box never creates a random code when left empty.

You can use a **4 to 16 digit number**, including one that starts with zero. Letters, numbers, symbols and spaces inside a code are allowed, up to 32 characters. Use at least 4 characters. English letters A and a work the same. Each person needs their own code. A code already used by another user cannot be used. If you choose one, the app says so and keeps your typed code so you can change it.

If your places are already saved in **My Areas**:

1. In **Android access → My phone → My areas**, choose **Create login code for My Areas**.
2. Review your saved Panchayats and Villages. Untick places you do not need. Search by place or group name; your other choices are kept.
3. Choose **Use selected areas**. Areas already added for the same Financial Year are reused. Current phone areas are kept.
4. Enter **My login code (optional)** and choose **Save my login code**, or leave it blank and choose **Generate my login code**.

Opening the shortcut does not add areas or change your code. A working code stays the same until you save a new code. Saving a new code ends earlier phone sign-ins. Secretary codes are kept separately.

If no areas are saved, choose **Open My Areas** and save your Panchayats or Villages first. Whole Districts and Blocks cannot be used in this shortcut. Areas need complete saved worker records and must be allowed for your signed-in account. Any problem is shown beside the affected areas. Confirmed areas are reused after a failed attempt. If My Areas changes during review, choose **Read saved areas** before continuing. An assigned area's Financial Year is kept; review that area before using a different year.

For other areas, **Add my Panchayat or Village** remains available. For Android access or changing areas manually:

1. Add your working Panchayats or Villages if they are not listed.
2. Choose **Set up my Android access**, or **Change my Android access** if already set up.
3. Search the assigned areas. Tick the areas you need. You can select several areas together; filtering keeps your choices.
4. Enter your preferred code in **My login code (optional)** in the code section, then choose **Save my login code**.
5. You can leave the box empty and choose **Generate my login code** instead. Saving a code ends earlier phone sign-ins and keeps desktop sign-in and Secretary codes.
6. Keep the code private. Enter it in the Worker Demand Android app. Choose from the areas assigned to your account.

Open **More code options**, then use **Disable my Android code** when access should end. Read the confirmation before removing area assignments; removing access does not erase official portal records.

To remove your own assignments, choose **Set up my Android access** or **Change my Android access**, select the areas and choose **Remove selected areas**. Check the listed names before choosing **Remove areas**. You can remove only areas assigned to your signed-in account. Other users' areas are protected. Saved worker records, requests, completed Work Demands, Muster Rolls, Secretary accounts, login codes and history are kept. The window stays open while waiting and shows any problem there. A confirmed removal stays removed even if the next list read fails. A lost connection can leave the result uncertain, so read the message before trying again.

If records are missing, it explains what to download and keeps your entries for another try. After the download finishes, choose **Save code** again. Access is created only when you save. Saving access shares that worker list with the Secretary.

## 4. Use the desktop Browser

1. Open **Browser**, then open VBGRAMG with your chosen area.
2. Check the website and area before entering your User ID and Password.
3. Enter the **Security Code** yourself and choose **Login**.
4. On the matching official login page, **Save** or **Update** stores the login pair only when you choose it. **Login details** lets you change or delete the saved pair.

### Print, find and keyboard keys

**Print a page.** Choose **🖨 Print** in the Browser toolbar, or press **Ctrl+P**, to open the normal Windows print box for the page you see. The Work Allocation page keeps its own print window with every row (see section 8). While a portal task is running, Print waits until the task finishes.

**Find words on a page.** Choose **⌕** in the Browser toolbar, or press **Ctrl+F**. Type the words. **Enter** or **F3** goes to the next match, **Shift+Enter** or **Shift+F3** to the previous one, and **Esc** closes the find box. Printing and finding only read the page. They do not save, send or submit anything.

**Browser keys.** **Alt+←** goes back and **Alt+→** goes forward. **F5** or **Ctrl+R** reloads the page after asking you, because the portal may repeat its last request. **Ctrl+L** moves to the web address box. **Ctrl+** **+**, **Ctrl+−** and **Ctrl+0** change the page size as before.

**Other app screens.** **Ctrl+P** prints the open screen in light colours, without the side menu, and long lists print in full. In a window with its own **Print** button, such as the demand preview, **Ctrl+P** uses that button. **Ctrl+F** first moves to the screen's own search box when it has one; press **Ctrl+F** again to find words anywhere on the screen.

**Everywhere.** **Ctrl+C** copy, **Ctrl+X** cut, **Ctrl+V** paste, **Ctrl+A** select all, **Ctrl+Z** undo and **Ctrl+Y** redo work in the Browser page, text boxes, lists and settings, together with Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter, Esc, Delete, Backspace and the arrow keys. Right-click a text box or selected words for **Cut**, **Copy**, **Paste** and **Select all**. These keys are desktop app features; the web preview uses your web browser's own Print and Find.

The Browser is a desktop feature. The narrow preview shows its controls; it does not show a real official website or a logged-in session.

## 5. Find Job Cards and workers

1. Open **Job Cards**. Choose the Village and Financial Year.
2. Search by Job Card, member, household head or Village. Choose the Job Card status and saved check filters, then **Apply**.
3. Use **Show household members** beside a Job Card to see its workers and saved checks. Scroll the table sideways on a narrow screen for the other columns.
4. Use **Update from VBGRAMG** when records need updating. Keep an incomplete or unknown result open for review.

Job Card members share the household's days left. A missing total means **Not known**, not zero. The totals under the list count only the selected workers: **Job Cards selected**, **Members selected**, **Days requested**, and the shared **Days worked** and **Workdays left** of each selected Job Card (counted once). Every Job Card and worker has a serial number, which continues on the next page.

## 6. Make a work request on Android

The first time you sign in, a short **Welcome tour** (six screens) explains the worker list, choosing workers, checking them, **Send to office** and **History**. Use **Next** and **Back**. **Skip tour** or **Start using the app** closes it. To see it again, open **My account** and choose **Welcome tour**. It is in English and Hindi.

1. On **Home**, check the line at the top (it says whether records are connected and when they were checked) and your assigned Panchayat or Village. If requests are waiting for the office or drafts are saved on the phone, a short list appears; tap a line to open **History**. A DEO can choose among their assigned areas.

Choose **Recheck data** under the selected area's download progress to request fresh information for that Panchayat or Village and Financial Year. It keeps saved workers and entered demand details. The recheck button is also available after a download finishes. Sign in through the desktop app if private checks are needed.

The download panel returns when you reopen the app with a saved area. Job Card, worker, eKYC, ABPS and Aadhaar Availability counts appear directly under the progress bar. Counts update while the download runs. An unknown total stays unknown until the full list has been read.

Progress reads family members together to avoid repeated lookups. Shared day calculations still include all family members they need. Worker-check counts use the worker reader's matching rules. When a worker name, member number or Job Card number changes, an earlier answer can show **Update needed**. The earlier answer and its date stay saved. A reported No still counts as read; it does not mean that worker is eligible for Work Demand.

Deleting saved data or choosing **Remove from Queue** stops that area's download. Visiting the area keeps that choice. The panel shows **Download stopped** and explains the reason. Choose **Download records again** to restart only that Panchayat or Village for the selected Financial Year. Completed records are reused.

A confirmed **Aadhaar Availability: Yes** is kept for the same worker and Job Card. Later availability checks, including **Recheck data** and **Update Data**, skip that completed answer. No, missing, unknown, N/D and answers not yet confirmed as Yes are checked again when the area needs an update. Failed reads keep their waiting time. If one family member still needs an answer, the portal may require opening the whole Job Card page; the other members' completed Yes answers keep their original check time. Daily day-report reads also keep those Yes answers.

Job Card numbers, household and member details appear first, followed by Aadhaar Authentication, Aadhaar Seeding, ABPS and eKYC. Workers with all four Yes can prepare Work Demand. Days and other records follow, with actual Aadhaar Availability confirmation last. A slow household page does not hold back all the following cards; the existing limit of four public card requests is kept. Saved lists and their totals share the same short-lived display copy. The current checks used for demand review and official entry remain separate.

In **Settings → Records**, open **Download order · one area at a time**. Each area shows **Queued**, **Downloading**, **Paused**, **Completed** or **Failed**. Queued means it has not started its turn. Paused means progress is kept while another area has priority or downloading has been paused. Failed means a read needs another attempt; earlier saved records remain available.

The selected Panchayat or Village is also named beside its progress bar. Just below it, a compact download overview shows **Currently downloading**, **Paused** and **Queued**, with area names and the Financial Year. A Village includes its Panchayat name. Up to two names from each waiting list stay visible; choose **more** to see the rest. The display updates every few seconds without opening Settings.

The area records panel is small and steady. It shows the area name, a large download percentage, a progress bar and one short message. Below are small cards for each saved record: Job Cards and worker details, eKYC, ABPS, Aadhaar seeding, Aadhaar authentication, days worked and days left, Aadhaar Availability and Work Codes, each with its own percentage. eKYC, ABPS, Aadhaar seeding and Aadhaar authentication come from one portal report, so the app reads them together in one pass. Aadhaar Availability follows from them: a worker with all four Yes counts as Aadhaar Availability: Yes for now, and the Work Demand form check confirms it next. A status that flips for a moment while the app retries is not shown until it has lasted a few seconds, so the panel does not blink. Choose **Show more** to read a long message in full.

**Waiting to download** means the request is saved but no page for that area is being read yet. **Download paused** means you must choose to continue. **Download incomplete** keeps saved records and shows that more checks are needed. A saved request alone does not show a running download. When no page is being read, an unknown total stays unknown and the progress bar does not move. Read **Record updates** for report problems. A worker check can also explain that it is waiting for sign-in or for a portal form to finish.

When you switch areas, **Finishing current page** names the previous area if a page is still loading, and **Next** names your new selection. Its percentage stays separate from the selected area's progress. Once the new area's turn finishes, paused downloads resume automatically with their saved records. A deliberate pause or a removed download still needs your choice to restart. If totals are unknown, the panel says **Checking totals** instead of inventing a percentage. If its status cannot be refreshed, the panel labels the last reported download and tries again.

Opening **Browser** to sign in keeps the area from the screen you just used. In a list with several ticked Villages, the last ticked Village owns the download turn. While a named report area is being matched to the saved location list, downloads wait instead of reading another area. A tab with no selected area releases the area hold. A deliberate download pause stays paused.

The first records are matching Job Card and worker details plus all four worker checks. All four Yes allow a temporary Aadhaar Availability Yes for preparation while the form check waits its turn. Each selected worker must still pass the required checks. An actual No replaces the temporary answer and shows the reason. Day figures and work lists remain optional for Work Demand. Requested extra downloads keep their place in the selected area's queue. Being ready to check a demand does not mean every report is complete.

Work Codes and reported Work Order numbers follow worker and day reads, before the final Aadhaar Availability confirmation. Work Codes come from a public report that needs no login. If the selected area is still waiting for worker checks that need a VBGRAMG login, its Work Codes download anyway. Saved Work Codes appear in Work Demand while other reports continue. The app reads every offered work-report page before saving one shared Panchayat list. The list is reused across its Villages. Different works may have the same Work Order number; their Work Codes stay separate. A number not supplied by the portal stays unavailable. Choosing a work that needs a current check can request its work report sooner.

### Manage downloaded data

1. Open **Settings → Manage downloaded data**. Choose the State, Financial Year, District and Block. Use **Update lists** if the offered place list needs refreshing.
2. Read each area's **Status**, **Last updated** and **Size**. **Fully downloaded** needs saved completion checks for the chosen year; a Job Card count alone is not enough. **Size** shows saved Job Card and member counts for all years on this computer. **Date unavailable** means the saved copy has no usable date. **Queued**, **Downloading** and **Interrupted** appear beside the saved-data status.
3. Tick individual Panchayats or Villages, or choose **Select All – Fully Downloaded**, **Select All – Partial**, **Select All – Interrupted/Incomplete**, **Select All – Queued**, or **Select All**. Status selections cover matching areas throughout this Block, even when search hides some names. Villages with other statuses stay unticked. You can still tick or untick any Village afterward. **Clear selection** starts again. Areas with neither saved data nor a queued download are left out of these group selections.
4. For one area, choose **Delete** on its row. For several, choose **Delete Selected**. Review the names and record counts in one confirmation, then choose **Delete Selected**. This removes downloaded Job Cards, members, checks and day records for **all Financial Years**. Matching downloaded work details and report copies are included. A private recovery copy is checked first. **Cancel** keeps the data. You do not need to read a missing Village list first.
5. To stop waiting downloads while keeping saved records, choose **Remove from Queue** on a row, or select several areas and use **Remove from Queue** above the list. Only selected areas still in the queue are included. Check the names and confirm once. A page already being read can finish. Removed areas stay stopped after reopening the app until you choose **Download selected** or **Recheck selected** again. Restarting one Village keeps its stopped siblings out of the queue.

Confirming **Delete Selected** pauses all downloads automatically. The app stops new pages and waits for the current page to return before checking the private recovery copy and deleting. Downloads stay paused afterward, including after reopening the app. Use **Download** or **Recheck** when you want to start again. Deleted areas stay stopped until you choose them again. If a page takes too long to stop, records are kept and the same confirmation offers another try.

Official submissions, demand history, phone requests, login details, area choices and exported files are kept. A running Work Demand entry or another official portal task must still finish or be stopped before deletion.

For a single area, choose **Download** directly on its row to continue missing records, or **Recheck** for fresh information for that Panchayat or Village and the chosen Financial Year. Saved records and confirmed Aadhaar Availability Yes answers are kept. A stopped area starts again; other stopped Villages stay stopped. You do not need to tick the row first. For several areas, use **Download selected** or **Recheck selected**. A whole Panchayat can be downloaded before its Village list is read. **Read Village list** is needed only to choose individual Villages that are not already listed. Downloads proceed one Village at a time while you use other app screens.

Saved records are shared across the app. Opening another tab, changing a filter or choosing another Village in a Panchayat reuses the information already saved for that area and year. A complete saved work list also survives reopening the app. Another download is needed only for missing, incomplete or outdated information, or when you ask for an update. Different report types still need their own report data.

Unrelated background updates do not cancel the deletion confirmation. If the selected records change while it is open, the same confirmation reads the latest counts and tells you to check them. Nothing is deleted until you confirm those updated details.

Removed areas stay stopped across restarts until you choose to download them again. A recheck keeps confirmed Aadhaar Availability Yes answers. Deleting an area's downloaded checks removes those local answers too; a fresh download must read them again. A family with manually saved members needs review before its downloaded Job Card can be deleted.

## 11. Balance and Recharge

Open **Balance & recharges** from the side menu. **Balance** is the app credit used for charged tasks. Recharge prices are shown in rupees. You can see your available balance, paid balance, free balance and any balance held for unfinished tasks.

A low balance never stops a Work Demand task. Village reports show a **Recharge** button when the selected reports need more balance. A low-balance answer while starting or saving a report also opens Recharge. Your current request and report choices are kept.

1. Read the balance and recharge price.
2. Choose an amount: ₹50, ₹100, ₹150, ₹200, ₹250, ₹300, ₹400, ₹500 or ₹1,000. Or type any whole-rupee amount from ₹50 to ₹1,000 in **Other amount**. No amount is chosen until you choose one. Each ₹1 you pay adds exactly 1 Balance, so ₹150 adds 150 and ₹1,000 adds 1,000. Choose **Recharge** and confirm before opening Razorpay. The Razorpay page always asks for the amount you chose. If the payment service does not confirm that amount, the app does not open Razorpay and no money is taken.
3. In the desktop app, Razorpay opens in a payment window inside the app. The window title shows which site is open, such as your bank's page. Complete the payment yourself, then close the window. The app checks the payment at once, and closes the window by itself when Razorpay reports the payment first. The app adds balance only after the payment has been checked. The message names the amount paid and the Balance added, for example "Payment of ₹150.00 checked. 150 was added to your balance once." If you close the window without paying, choose **Reopen this recharge** to pay later. **Check payment** in the list still works at any time.
4. Choose **Return to your work**. Review the kept details, check the balance and choose the next task action yourself.

If an earlier recharge is not finished, the app offers **Reopen this recharge** at its first amount. Choose **Choose another amount** to start a new one instead. The earlier recharge stays in the list, and **Check payment** still works for it.

A successful Work Demand entry uses 1 from your Balance for every 20 selected workers. A part of 20 counts as a full 20:

| Selected workers | Balance used |
| --- | --- |
| 1–20 | 1 |
| 21–40 | 2 |
| 81–100 | 5 |
| 101–120 | 6 |
| 281–300 | 15 |

Before entry starts, the app sets this amount aside from your available Balance. It is used only after every saved Work Demand is confirmed. The entry progress panel shows the amount set aside and, at the end, the amount used. A failed run releases its reservation. A run with an unclear result keeps its reservation until its saved result is checked. A funded run can continue using that reservation; it does not pay twice. If a run stops part-way and is not continued, you pay only for the workers whose demand was saved and checked: 1 for every 20 of them, never more than the amount set aside. The rest is released. This happens when you start another Work Demand task, or when nobody has continued the run for a day. If you later continue the same list, workers already paid for are not charged again. A run that set aside Balance at an older price keeps that price.

Each time Balance is used for Work Demand, the bell in the top bar shows a **Balance used for Work Demand** notice. It says how much was used, for how many saved and checked workers, in which area, any part owed and the Balance left. A new notice also appears as a Windows notification, so you see it while the app is in the background. Choose **Mark as read** to remove it. The same charge is also listed in your balance history.

If the available Balance is too low, entry still starts and runs normally. There is no message and no stop. The app sets aside what is available and records the rest as owed. For example, 298 workers use 15; with 5 available, 5 is set aside and 10 is owed. The progress panel shows both parts. If the run fails, nothing is used and nothing is owed. If every demand is saved and confirmed, the owed part becomes an **Amount owed** on **Balance & recharges**. Your next recharge, or any other Balance added later, pays the amount owed first, automatically. The rest is added to your Balance. Your balance history shows this as **Amount owed – taken from new balance**. An older copy of the app must be updated before it can start a new charged run. Completed free work keeps its original price. If the balance cannot be checked, entry waits until it can be checked.

A successfully saved Village report uses 2, including each Village in a whole Panchayat. Failed reports and cancelled saves cost nothing. Viewing a cost or opening Recharge does not start a payment.

## When something needs attention

| Message or result | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Area or Financial Year unavailable | Read the current lists and choose an offered value. Check that the portal account has access. |
| Saved records incomplete | Keep the saved records, update the selected area and read the result again. |
| Worker check unknown or old | Update the checks. Unknown does not mean Yes, No or zero. |
| Aadhaar available not confirmed Yes | Review that worker's portal answer before portal entry. Do not use a filter to bypass the check. |
| Request changed elsewhere | Read the latest changes, review the workers and check again. |
| Portal reply uncertain | Check the saved list before retrying. |
| Saved records changed. Check the demand again. | The notice lists each worker in this demand whose saved record changed, and how. Choose **Check demand again**. Your workers, days and dates are kept. A change to another worker in the Village does not clear your checked demand. |
| Need your check (during entry) | Choose **See why … workers need your check**. Each worker shows the earlier entry that holds it, its dates and what to do. A held worker is never sent twice. |
| Saving a list of workers with issues | Choose **Save as PDF** or **Save as Excel** below a worker issue list. The file is a list to check or print. It is not an official record and sends nothing to VBGRAMG. |
| Balance too low | Open Recharge, check the amount and decide whether to add balance. Your current work is kept. |
| Phone offline | Keep the same saved request and reconnect. Check History for its received status. |

## 12. Choose your language

Both apps can show English, Hindi and Garo.

- **Android:** choose **Language** on the sign-in screen or in **My account**. The choice is kept on the phone. Sign-in, Home, Choose workers, Request details, Review and History have Hindi text.
- **Desktop:** choose **Language** at the top of **Settings → Appearance**. The menu, top bar, footer and the Work Demand page have Hindi text. Other desktop screens are still being added and show English.

Hindi is a first draft. **Garo is a draft translation**: it has no words yet, so the app shows English and says so until a Garo speaker has confirmed the text. Names, numbers, dates, amounts and official names such as Job Card, Work Order and Muster Roll are not translated.
