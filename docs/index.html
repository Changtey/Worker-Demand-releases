On Android: start in three steps

  1. Download and installOpen the Android file on your phone. When updating, keep the existing app and saved drafts.
  2. Enter your Android login codeAsk your DEO for your assigned code. Check your account and assigned area after signing in.
  3. Prepare a work requestChoose workers, enter days and dates, then review and send the request to the office.

On your computer: start in three steps

  1. Download and installChoose the desktop installer for your computer.
  2. Sign in with GoogleOpen VBGRAMG DEO Assistant and use your own Google account.
  3. Complete your registrationEnter your Full Name and Block Name. If a DEO invited you, enter their referral code before finishing registration. It cannot be added later.

Your inviter receives 50 in free balance after your first completed, checked Work Demand that uses free balance. Downloading or signing up alone does not earn their reward.
