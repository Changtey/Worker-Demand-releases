## First setup and daily records

- **Menus stay available:** open every app menu before portal sign-in, during setup or after portal sign-out. Setup stays in Browser. Portal checks and entry still need the right signed-in account.
- **Work Demand opens after sign-in:** a confirmed portal sign-in opens **Create demand** immediately.
- **Two setup steps:** Select login area → Sign in → Start Work Demand Creation. Setup does not select work areas or start record or Muster Roll downloads. Older unfinished setup downloads cannot block access.
- **Personal default login page:** choose your State, Financial Year, District and Block on **Login area**. **Save and open login page** applies those choices in Browser and moves to **Sign in**. Enter the official User ID, password and CAPTCHA yourself. The app waits for the form to be ready and continues while other images load. Any opening problem is explained in setup, with the saved choices kept for retry.
- **Secure saving after sign-in:** choose to save the login pair on the Login area form. The original User ID and password are saved securely only after a confirmed sign-in. Failed logins keep the earlier saved pair. CAPTCHA is never saved. Save, Update and Login details remain available in Browser.
- **Choose areas when working:** select the area for each demand in Work Demand. Use **My Areas** to save places you work on often.
- **Separate record tools:** use **Settings → Records** to download or update area records. Use **Reports** for Muster Roll reports. These are separate from setup.
- **Reuse saved records:** current saved records make later area work faster. Missing records are checked when needed.
- **Reasons for stopped progress:** missing worker checks name their Village. Worker reads explain unreadable Yes or No answers, unmatched worker details, missing Job Cards and member lists that did not load. A disagreeing Muster Roll summary keeps its warning while checked source reports are saved and reused on retry.
- **Daily background updates:** area records can update while the app is open and connected. Your current area comes first. Completed records are reused, portal entry takes priority, and a deliberate pause is kept. Personal sign-in and report Security Codes still need your answer.
- **Keep saved work during updates:** settings, selected areas, downloaded records, drafts and securely saved login details stay outside installation files. Normal app updates retain them and do not repeat completed first setup.
- **Windows app updates:** from version 1.4.18, one **Update now** choice downloads and checks the file, starts installation and opens the updated app after installation. A failed download or start can be tried again without reopening the app. Earlier versions need version 1.4.18 installed once to receive this updater.
- **Reuse completed worker checks:** eKYC and ABPS keep their existing reuse rules. A confirmed Aadhaar Availability **Yes** is kept for the same worker and Job Card, including during **Recheck data** and **Update Data**. No, unknown, missing and unconfirmed availability answers are tried again when the area needs an update. A changed worker or Job Card must have its own matching proof.

Saved selections do not change official permissions. Source downloads do not submit demands or create paid PDF copies.

- **Record download progress:** request rows and open requests show a clear bar and measured percentage. Unknown totals stay unknown. Pauses, failures and sign-in problems keep their status; 100% still needs the existing record checks to finish.
- **Automatic downloads:** the selected Panchayat or Village downloads first and alone, in a fixed order, and retries on its own within two minutes. Completed, current records are kept and reused. Other areas wait until the selected area is complete. Entered demand details stay in place.
- **Recheck data:** request fresh information for the selected Panchayat or Village and year, including after a download finishes. Saved records and confirmed Aadhaar Availability **Yes** answers are kept. Repeated clicks share the same pending request.
- **Manage downloaded data:** each Panchayat and Village has **Recheck** and **Delete** buttons, download status, last saved date and record counts. Recheck starts fresh area reads for the chosen year while keeping saved records and confirmed Aadhaar Availability Yes answers. Select fully downloaded, partial, interrupted/incomplete or queued areas with one button, or use **Select All**. Adjust individual ticks, then **Delete Selected** or **Remove from Queue** and confirm once. Status selections include matching areas hidden by search and keep Villages with other statuses unticked. Queue removal keeps saved records and stays in effect after reopening the app. A page already being read can finish safely. Deletion covers all years, checks a private recovery copy first and works even before a Village list is read. Official demand history, phone requests, login details, area choices and exported files are kept. Download and recheck controls stay on the same screen.
- **Factory reset:** Settings has a separate full reset for the desktop app. After confirmation, it stops local downloads and the local service, clears downloaded records, all settings and areas, drafts, local request and entry history, recovery copies, passwords and sign-ins for all app accounts on this computer, then creates empty records and reopens at first setup. Cancel keeps the records. Active Work Demand entry must finish or stop first. Another app copy using these records blocks reset before removal starts. An interrupted reset finishes before normal startup. Online account data and official portal records stay in place; files exported outside the app stay in place.

- **Download once and reuse:** every area row also has **Download** for missing records. Saved Job Cards, worker checks, day figures and work lists are read from the same local records across screens and filters. A complete saved work list is shared by the Panchayat's Villages and remains usable after reopening the app. Location lists are also shared with background readers. Missing, incomplete, outdated or manually refreshed information can be downloaded again. Different reports still need their own information; a worker check cannot replace an attendance report.
- **Clear deletion checks:** unrelated background updates no longer cancel a deletion review. If the selected records change, the same confirmation shows updated counts and asks you to check them before deleting. Records are kept until you confirm the current selection.
- **Delete stops downloads:** confirming **Delete Selected** pauses downloads, stops new pages and waits for the current page before deleting. Downloads stay paused after reopening. A slow page keeps the records for another try. Work Demand entry and official portal tasks still need to finish or be stopped first.
- **Less waiting between household pages:** a slow public Job Card page does not stop the following cards from being read. At most four public card requests run together. Checked households are saved in small groups and incomplete downloads remain clearly marked.

## Simple daily work

Desktop and Android use short labels, clear work steps and details that open when needed. Work Demand opens after setup and remembers Create demand or Received requests. Desktop sends checked demands to VBGRAMG; Android sends requests to the office. Settings use Work, App and Account groups with a section chooser on narrow screens. All existing checks, filters, reports, data and permissions remain in place.

## Worker checks

The desktop Job Cards list shows **Eligible**, **Not eligible**, **Not yet checked** or **Data unavailable** for each saved worker. Every required check must pass before **Eligible** appears. Optional day figures do not add a new requirement. These labels describe saved records; **Check demand** still performs the checks needed for the current request.

Android shows a small tag beside a worker only when something needs attention: **Inactive**, **Status unknown**, **eKYC pending** or **eKYC unknown**. A short line above the list says when every listed worker is active with eKYC complete. The same tags appear when entering days and reviewing a request. Set **Worker filters → Active member → All** to include inactive workers. Android can read public portal reports directly for selected assigned workers. Use **Check portal**, then the worker's information button for **Worker details**, including each answer's source and check time. It reads explicit eKYC, active or inactive status, offered identity and payment checks, complete worked days and confirmed family days left. Missing, incomplete or access-protected reports keep the saved copy with a clear message. Phone checks do not use Balance or submit official records.

The app shows saved answers and whether they are current. Electronic identity verification (**eKYC**) and the Aadhaar Based Payment System (**ABPS**) are shown separately from Aadhaar availability. Statuses such as inactive, deleted, missing or old remain meaningful. Missing information is never counted as a confirmed answer.

Filters help organise the list. They do not waive the checks needed for portal entry. Members of a Job Card share its annual days left; this is optional information, not a separate allowance for each worker or a limit on the days entered in the app.

Received requests show **Queued**, **Downloading**, **Ready**, **Partial data**, **Failed**, or **Attention required** for their records. Ready means the required saved records are current; the demand still needs its own check. Inactive workers and workers with missing identity or payment answers stay saved and searchable. Missing answers stay unknown. Private reports need a permitted VBGRAMG sign-in in the desktop app. Downloads respect a deliberate pause, keep completed records, and retry temporary failures without submitting demands.

## Locations across India

In **My Areas → Frequently used areas**, tick individual places or choose **Select all**, then **Remove selected**. **Remove all** clears the current list. Both ask for one confirmation showing the area names. **Clear selection** unticks the list. Saved records and My Areas groups are kept. Removed areas stay removed after another visit; **Show removed priorities → Use as a priority** adds them again.

The app uses the portal's current State, District, Block, Panchayat and Village lists. It uses the Financial Years and areas offered to the current account. Examples in pictures are fictional and are not built-in location restrictions. Live coverage of every State has not been established by these screen checks.

Work Demand, Secretary login codes and Job Cards share stable area choices. Worker updates and Android request checks reuse the unchanged area lists. Update lists keeps the earlier choices visible while reading. A late reply for an earlier selection cannot replace the area you just chose.

Downloads follow the selected Panchayat or Village and Financial Year in the tab being used. Its queued download or update stays ahead of other areas. A whole Panchayat includes its current offered Villages, read one at a time. Other areas continue afterward, one by one. Hidden tabs cannot replace the choice. Browser keeps the area from the screen just used during sign-in. Downloads keep completed records and respect a deliberate pause.

The Panchayat or Village you are currently working on gets the first turn for downloads and worker checks, including areas outside My Areas. A whole Panchayat gives its Villages the first turn. Changing area changes the next read and keeps completed records. Portal account limits, offered locations and deliberate pauses still apply. Each open app copy keeps its own portal sign-in and progress message.

Work Demand member lists and Work Order choices finish their current read before checking newer saved records. Background progress does not repeatedly restart the same read. Returning to Work Demand checks the latest lists; a Village still downloading is checked again automatically. Entered days and dates are kept.

During desktop Work Demand entry, the app finds each household by its full Registration ID (Job Card number), checks that it was chosen, and then finds the worker by name inside that household only. When the next worker is in the same Panchayat and Village, the app picks the next Registration ID directly, without choosing the Panchayat and Village again, but only when it is safe: the portal copies the last worker's dates into the same line of the next household, and that line must be the requested worker's own line with the same dates. Otherwise the app starts a fresh form and chooses the area again, as before. Households and their members are entered in the order that makes the direct choice possible most often, one Panchayat and Village at a time; the list on screen keeps its order. Received requests and manual entries use the same steps.

**Print and find in Browser.** **🖨 Print** in the Browser toolbar and **Ctrl+P** open the normal Windows print box for the page you see. **⌕** and **Ctrl+F** find words on the page, with the number of matches; **Enter** and **Shift+Enter** move between them. **Alt+←**, **Alt+→**, **F5** (after asking) and **Ctrl+L** work as in a normal web browser. Printing and finding only read the page.

**Print and find on app screens.** **Ctrl+P** prints the open screen in light colours, without the side menu, and long lists print in full. A window with its own Print button uses that button. **Ctrl+F** goes to the screen's search box first, then finds words anywhere on the screen. Copy, Cut, Paste, Select all, Undo and Redo work with the keyboard everywhere and from a right-click menu in text boxes and selected words.

**Print Work Allocation.** On the portal's Work Allocation page, the highlighted **🖨 Print** button in the Browser toolbar and **Ctrl+P** open the app's print window. A print window shows the real pages before printing, with printer, paper size, layout and copies, plus the normal Windows print box. The print includes the area and every row, also those below the screen, on as many pages as needed. Printing only reads the page; nothing is saved or allocated.

Batch entry uses fewer repeated browser reads and waits for each changing page to finish. It checks the current sign-in, compares the live form before filling and checks the full requested details again before each Proceed. Starting a batch checks saved entry records together; each worker still has separate duplicate protection. Local checks use fictional records. Live portal speed is not yet measured for these source changes.

Repeated checks reuse the private app connection to Browser. Lost replies never trigger an automatic resend through that connection. Idle connections are safely replaced for the next command. The worker checks, saved-detail visits and per-worker duplicate protection remain in place.

Ready forms continue without waiting for unrelated background pictures. Browser checks have a short deadline, and expired commands cannot press buttons later. Current page, worker and portal-response checks remain in place. A deliberate Pause is respected. This behaviour is checked in the actual desktop using fictional pages with an image that never finishes loading.
